Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information that you provide directly, including your name, email address, and password when you register for an account. We also collect the expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may also automatically collect certain technical information, such as your browser type, device information, and usage patterns within the app, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Generate expense summaries, charts, and insights for your personal use
  • Communicate with you about updates, issues, or changes to the Service
  • Improve and personalize your experience within Spendly

We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a secure database associated with your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party services (such as hosting providers or analytics tools) to operate the Service. These providers only have access to the information necessary to perform their functions and are obligated to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Spendly does not share your expense data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. Your Choices

You may access, update, or delete your account information at any time through your profile settings. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated data, please contact us using the information below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.app.
